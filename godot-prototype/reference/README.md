## What was structurally changed after looking at A

| A observation | Trial implementation | Still unverified or missing |
|---|---|---|
| A full village fills the view rather than separate floating props | 7 white-wall/tile-roof homes, straw warehouse, open wood shelter; fixed composed camera | Actual GPU composition and occlusion have not been seen |
| Warm earth ties thresholds, yards and paths together | 9 irregular soft-shouldered aprons, stone thresholds, connected road shoulders | No artist-painted unique courtyard/courtyard walls |
| The creek has depth, irregular edges, stones and bank plants | 5 bank/water bands, relief soil edge, grouped faceted stones and reeds; short stone crossing | Bridge/stone detail is code-native, not A-quality painted art |
| Trees form rear canopy, small garden groups and foreground framing | 35 chosen tree locations, mature/young silhouettes, orchard groups, foreground willows and low plants | Only a few reusable silhouettes; no true bamboo or full variant set |
| Objects share light and ground contact | Separate lower-right alpha-silhouette ground shadows, stone contact shading | Not a ray-traced or physically unified light system; source lighting remains baked |
| The UI is ivory paper and restrained jade | Compact bar, illustration cards, same-side confirmation, no invented resources | Real rendered typography/spacing/scroll acceptance remains outstanding |
| The scene feels inhabited | 7 moving animals; 5 static decorative human figures | No citizen AI or production activity; figures are only diorama props |

The implementation is a more useful **A-directed art experiment**, not proof
that switching engines alone achieves A. No paid generation, external art pack,
system setting change, original web entry change or production deployment was used.
